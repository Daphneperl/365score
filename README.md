# 365Scores

## Sports

### Strategy & Competitors
**Market Insights:** Most sports LPs are static app-store banners. People bounce. Better products win by personalizing early and building a bit of anticipation.

**Our Concept:** A 3-tap onboarding flow: pick sports, leagues, then teams. A short loading state sits between selection and the matchday feed so the result feels built for you, not dumped on you.

**The Advantage:** That brief wait makes the feed feel earned. Users stick around longer than they do on a generic banner LP.

### Next Steps
- **Live Odds & Stats:** Odds and live stats on fixture cards.
- **Calendar Sync:** Add upcoming matches to the device calendar.
- **Haptics:** Light vibration on each selection step.
- **Anticipated Matches:** Highlight the fixtures users are most looking forward to, upcoming and live, so those games stand out in the feed.

## Casino

### Strategy & Competitors
**Market Insights:** Casino LPs usually lead with a static bonus number. Gamified offers (wheels, scratch cards) convert better because the bonus feels won.

**Our Concept:** One spin. Land a guaranteed 600-coin package, framed as a jackpot with confetti, then claim via email and pick a game. Headlines A/B test challenge (“Think You Can Hit the Jackpot?”) vs. certainty (“Welcome Package Unlocked”).

**The Advantage:** The spin turns a flat welcome bonus into a moment. Outcome stays predictable; the ritual is what competitors’ CTAs miss.

### Next Steps
- **Persistent Balance:** Carry claimed coins into the first game.
- **Variant Analytics:** Track spin → claim → game tap for each headline.
- **Variant Wins:** A/B test different prize amounts on the wheel.
- **Sound & Haptics:** Spin ticks, a win sound, and light vibration.
