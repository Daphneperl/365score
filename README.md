# 365Scores

## Sports

### Strategy & Competitors
**Market Insights:** I see most sports LPs as static app-store banners that people bounce from. Score apps like SofaScore, FotMob, and Flashscore personalize after install through a settings-style pick of teams and leagues: useful, but rarely engaging. Betting apps (bet365, DraftKings, FanDuel) lean into gamified hooks such as odds boosts and streak bonuses, but those moments usually sit behind account creation. The gap I care about is the first visit: nobody turns the opening seconds into personalization before signup.

**My Concept:** I built a mobile-first, 3-tap onboarding flow where users pick sports, leagues, then teams. I put a short loading state between selection and the matchday feed so the result feels built for them, not dumped on them. Crests react on tap, the feed assembles on reveal, and a light reward moment (badge + confetti) closes the loop without requiring an account.

**The Advantage:** I borrow fast personalization from FotMob/SofaScore, but make every tap visibly build the live feed instead of saving a preference. I borrow the reward instinct from betting promos, but front-load it to the pre-signup landing so it converts, not only retains. That brief wait makes the feed feel earned, and I expect users to stick around longer than they do on a generic banner LP.

### Next Steps
- **Live Odds & Stats:** I want odds and live stats on fixture cards.
- **Calendar Sync:** I want users to add upcoming matches to the device calendar.
- **Haptics:** I want light vibration on each selection step.
- **Anticipated Matches:** I want to highlight the matches users are most looking forward to, upcoming and live, so those games stand out in the feed.

## Casino

### Strategy & Competitors
**Market Insights:** I see casino LPs usually leading with a static bonus number and a hard claim CTA. Competitors either provide the bonus/coupons that do not feel won. Won bonuses convert better than one that feels handed over, having users "test their luck" and thus more engaged. additionaly, many still gate the payoff behind signup forms that kill momentum.

**My Concept:** I designed one spin that lands a guaranteed 600-coin package, framed as a jackpot with confetti, then claim via email and pick a game. I A/B test headlines between challenge (“Think You Can Hit the Jackpot?”) and certainty (“Welcome Package Unlocked”). The outcome stays predictable; the ritual is the product.

**The Advantage:** I use the spin to turn a flat welcome bonus into a moment competitors’ plain CTAs miss. Guaranteed value keeps trust high, while the wheel, confetti, and claim step create ownership before the user ever opens a game. I place email capture and game choice after the win, when intent is highest.

### Next Steps
- **Persistent Balance:** I want claimed coins to carry into the first game.
- **Variant Analytics:** I want to track spin → claim → game tap for each headline.
- **Variant Wins:** I want to A/B test different prize amounts on the wheel.
- **Sound & Haptics:** I want spin ticks, a win sound, and light vibration.
